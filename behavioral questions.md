
### 1. Making a Decision Others Disagreed With

- **Main Question:** Describe a situation where you made an important technical or product decision that was unpopular with your team or stakeholders, and you still moved forward with it.
    
- **Follow-up Questions:**
    
    - What data or reasoning led you to your decision?
        
    - How did you communicate your rationale to those who disagreed?
        
    - Looking back, was the decision correct? What would you change about the process?
        

### 2. Message Broadcast

- **Main Question:** Describe a time when you had to share a difficult message or update with a broad audience (e.g., your team, department, or company).
    
- **Follow-up Questions:**
    
    - How did you prepare for the communication?
        
    - What was the reaction, and how did you handle questions or concerns?
        
    - What was the outcome, and what did you learn from the experience?
        

### 3. Tell Me About a Challenging Project

- **Main Question:** Tell me about a challenging and interesting project you did recently.
    
- **Follow-up Question:** What problems did you encounter?
    

### 4. Complex Project and Resolving Team Conflicts

- **Main Question:** Describe a complex project and how you resolved conflicts between teammates.
    

### 5. Conflict Management and Stakeholder Expectations

- **Main Question:** Interview focusing on: Mediating conflicts at work, Managing expectations with management and stakeholders. (Conducted by a Director).
    

### 6. Conflict Resolution with Uncooperative Colleagues

- **Main Question:** How do you solve issues with uncooperative colleagues?
    

### 7. Googlyness Behavioral: Conflict, Multitasking, Help Others (45 min)

- **Main Questions:**
    
    - How would you handle conflicts?
        
    - How do you manage multiple projects simultaneously?
        
    - Have you gone out of your way to help someone outside your team?
        
- **Follow-up Question:** How do you scope work? (scoping vs approvals vs dev time)
